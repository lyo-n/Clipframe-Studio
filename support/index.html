FAQ

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Screenshots, screen recording up to 1 minute and the color picker are free. Pro adds the annotation editor, smart object cut-out, text recognition, GIF recording and unlimited recording length.

Where are my screenshots and recordings saved?

To the folder shown in Settings → General → Save to folder (⌘V still copies straight to your clipboard too). Click Change… to pick a different folder.

Can I change the keyboard shortcuts?

Yes — in Settings → Screenshots, Video and Color Picker, click a shortcut field and press a new key combination.

Does Clipframe Studio send my data anywhere?

No. Clipframe Studio has no accounts or analytics. Screenshots, recordings, recognized text and transcripts stay on your Mac. The only download is a speech model, when you choose one; nothing of yours is sent. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I manage or cancel my Pro subscription?

Use the Manage Subscription button in Settings → Pro, or your App Store account settings.

How do I change the app language?

In Settings → General → Language. Clipframe Studio supports 12 languages.
